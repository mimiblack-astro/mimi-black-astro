Release the old cycle – and step consciously into the dark half of the year

Long before October 31 became modern Halloween, it was Samhain – the Celtic New Year and one of the great fire festivals of the Wheel of the Year. It marks the end of the harvest, the night when “the veil is thin”, and the start of the quiet 7-week corridor towards the Winter Solstice (Yule, December 21) and the 12 Sacred Nights.

This English-language ritual e-book and guided practice workbook walks you step by step through a calm, safe and meaningful Samhain evening: you let go of what is complete, honor your roots, and plant a secret “Winter Seed” intention that matures until the Solstice.

The 5 steps of your Samhain ritual

Step 1 · Circle of Protection

Cleanse your space with smoke or sound and open a clear, protected ritual space (Metal element · boundaries).

Step 2 · Honoring the Ancestors

A gentle ritual of grateful separation: receive the gifts of your lineage and respectfully return old burdens (Water element · roots).

Step 3 · Cauldron Fire Release

Name what is ready to end with the old cycle and hand it over to the transforming flame – with guided invocation (Fire element).

Step 4 · Threshold Oracle

A 3-position spread for Tarot, oracle cards, the 24 Elder Futhark runes or pure intuitive imagery, looking towards the Winter Solstice.

Step 5 · Your Secret Winter Seed

Word and seal one quiet intention on October 31 and open it again on December 21 (Earth element · sacred incubation).

Plus: Journals & Roadmap

Dream & omen journal for the 3 nights of the thin veil, Winter Solstice check-in, and your roadmap Samhain → Yule → 12 Sacred Nights.
